# AIGC-LipSync Benchmark

## 📋 Overview

**AIGC-LipSync Benchmark** is a comprehensive evaluation benchmark specifically designed for lip synchronization in AI-Generated Content (AIGC). This benchmark consists of 615 high-quality videos covering a wide spectrum of visual representations, from realistic humans to stylized characters, enabling thorough assessment of lip synchronization methods across diverse AI-generated video scenarios.

## 🎯 Key Features

### Dataset Composition

- **Total Videos**: 615 video clips
- **Average Duration**: ~6 seconds
- **Average Resolution**: 976×1409 pixels
- **Average Frame Rate**: 30 FPS

## 🎬 Video Sources

All video materials are generated by leading text-to-video (T2V) and image-to-video (I2V) models:

- **Kling**
- **JiMeng**
- **Wan**
- **Hunyuan**

Videos are collected from publicly accessible community platforms such as Civitai, ensuring content diversity and representativeness.

## ⚠️ Ethical Statement

This benchmark is created to advance scientific research and evaluation of lip synchronization technology. We emphasize that this technology should be applied to beneficial areas such as creative production, accessibility services, and educational content. We encourage the research community to continue developing detection methods for synthetically modified content alongside improvements in generation quality.

When using this dataset, please comply with relevant laws and regulations, and do not use it to create misleading or deceptive media content.
